## Ranked findings

1. **High — financial amounts break inside digits at 320px.** In the six-loan state, `$46,560.00` and `$15,408.18` break before the last digit; active scenario values do likewise. This impairs scanning and can cause misreading. `src/components/LoanTrajectory.vue:80` uses three equal columns and line 84 uses `overflow-wrap: anywhere`. At a narrow breakpoint use a two-column grid or allow metric items to wrap into rows, and keep each numeric amount `white-space: nowrap`; keep labels associated. Confirm longer amounts without horizontal overflow. See `terra-six-320.png`, `terra-hidden-320.png`.
2. **Medium — touch targets are small.** `src/components/LoanList.vue:147` forces 32px icon buttons at widths ≤352px, overriding its coarse-pointer 44px rule; on the desktop-pointer captures they are also 32px at 375px and 768px. Date and money nudge controls are 36px (`DateRuler.vue:109`, `MoneyRuler.vue:41`), and the switch is 44×36 (`PaymentControls.vue:65`). Preserve the compact icons and visual treatment but make actual hit boxes at least 44×44 on coarse pointers, adjusting spacing or layout to avoid overlap. Verify at 320px with touch emulation. This is a motor-accessibility issue, not a request for larger artwork.
3. **Medium — long loan names are not identifiable from the list.** The synthetic name is ellipsized at every width including desktop; similarly prefixed names could be indistinguishable. `src/components/LoanList.vue` truncates the name in the compact card. Allow two lines where needed, or provide a keyboard- and touch-accessible way to reveal the full name without entering edit mode; retain the eye controls and card layout. Screenshot `terra-six-375.png`. The edit dialog exposes the name, but requires a separate action and the focused input initially shows its end.
4. **Low — chart title is squeezed on narrow screens.** `src/components/BalanceChart.vue:137` always reserves half the header for the time badge, so “Balance over time” wraps at 320px even with no badge. Use `auto minmax(0, auto)` or flex with a nonshrinking title and wrap the badge only when necessary; allow header height to grow. This improves hierarchy without changing the chart or adding copy. See `terra-insufficient-320.png` and `terra-hidden-320.png`.
5. **Low — mobile results are buried below repeated whitespace.** At 375px six cards occupy roughly 830px before repayment metrics; screenshot height is 2,183px for the initial six-loan state. Keep the 44px payment inputs and touch targets, but consider a modest reduction in card vertical padding or the gap between the name/balance group and payment row, checking long names and allocation tags. Avoid collapsing cards or moving the chart ahead of the loans without user testing. At desktop and tablet the multi-column grid already manages density well.

## Tradeoffs and observations

The restrained palette and clear black/red/green repayment bar work well; the segmented metrics should stay textual as well as colored. The date card sits above separate money cards, with no date ticks; the $5 ruler notches and exact-entry inputs are consistent with the established design. Do not shrink targets or add explanatory text to save space. The empty state is spacious and clear; its large desktop whitespace is a stylistic choice, not a defect. The insufficient-payment state clearly marks the loan and explains the missing estimate, though the narrow metric layout magnifies its wrapping issues. Modal fields are comfortably sized and the dialog scrolls within the viewport; at 375px its action row is partly below the initial fold, which is reasonable given field count.

## Method and limits

Read the current source and verified HEAD `1071e30`. Inspected full-page screenshots at 320, 375, 768 and 1280 CSS pixels in isolated headless Chromium with representative synthetic localStorage fixtures: empty, six loans including a long name, add/edit modal, active $500 one-time plus $100 monthly scenario, hidden loan, and one loan with an insufficient $1 monthly payment. Also captured a normal one-loan state at 375px. Checked no document-level horizontal overflow for the six-loan scenario at all four widths; clicked an eye, the one-time inclusion switch, and the date earlier button (March to February 2033); confirmed the excluded amount input disabled. Checked modal Escape and initial focus visually. Browser contexts were closed. Captures are headless viewport simulations, not native mobile-device tests. Drag/swipe momentum, full keyboard traversal, screen-reader output, contrast measurement, exhaustive form validation, calculation correctness and every combination were not independently tested; do not infer they pass. Screenshot rendering uses the host fonts and a fixed 850px viewport height.
